Before you submit

Driver terms & conditions

1. Bookings only through Rydanu. If a traveler found you via Rydanu, the tour must be booked and paid through Rydanu. Arranging payment privately to bypass the platform is not allowed and can lead to account suspension.

2. Fees. A small platform fee applies per confirmed booking, based on the tour price — see "Fees & billing" in your dashboard. No bookings, no fees.

3. Cancellations. If a guest doesn't show up, you keep the deposit and owe no fee for that booking. If you cancel a confirmed tour yourself, a fixed cancellation fee of IDR 30,000 applies and the guest is refunded in full.

4. Keep your profile accurate. Vehicle, documents, service regions and prices must reflect reality. Update your listing whenever your vehicle or availability changes.

5. Conduct. Be respectful, punctual and safe. Repeated cancellations, poor reviews or safety issues can lead to suspension or removal from Rydanu.

6. Payments. Deposits and payments are handled via the payment details you provide (QRIS / payment link). Rydanu is not liable for errors in the details you submit.
